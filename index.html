Workflow

Three steps. Zero friction.

01 · COPY

Copy in Elementor

Right-click any element and click Copy (Cmd+C), or hit Copy Entire Page in the popup.

02 · SWITCH

Open Target Domain

Switch to another Elementor editor tab — local, staging, or production. The active block syncs automatically.

03 · PASTE

Paste Instantly

Press Cmd+V or choose Paste inside Elementor. Layout, styles, and structure appear right away.

Capabilities

Built specifically for Elementor workflows.

One-Click Full Page Copy

Grab every top-level section and Flexbox/Grid container from the active page in a single click without saving templates.

Recent Blocks History

Stores your last 10 copied blocks locally so you can switch between sections, columns, and widgets anytime.

Clean Media Sanitization

Strips mismatched foreign WordPress attachment IDs automatically so pasted blocks never corrupt target media metadata.

Private by Architecture

Runs 100% inside your browser via chrome.storage.local. Zero external requests, analytics, or servers.
